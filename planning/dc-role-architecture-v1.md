# Management
- Metrics tracked per staff + dept
- Full access . all portals . all tickets . perf metrics per staff + dept

**Mgmt metrics:**
- Deadline hit rate
- Attendance rate
- Task completion %
- Tickets open/close
- Avg resolution time
- Budget spent/dept
- Staff activity score
- Weekly trend graph

- HR Dept

- IT Dept

## Teacher
- Teacher (teal)

"Ambassador" "Parent" "Student" "Student portal . raises tickets routed to dept"

**Ticket/task system – all tasks are tickets**

**Attrs:**
- Title
- assignee
- dept
- deadline
- priority
- status
- comments thread
- file attachments

"Student/Parent → Route to any dept . PR/TA can forward to Teacher for comment → Teacher replies → staff close IT/Marketing cannot see student tickets unless forwarded to their dept"

"Staff → Create internal tickets/tasks . assign to member . forward interdept . escalate to management Ambassador → tickets to DC staff only (any dept)"

  - Supervisor + management see all depts
