IT/Marketing cannot see student tickets unless forwarded to their dept

Staff → create internal tickets/tasks · assign to member · forward interdept · escalate to mgmt

Ambassador → tickets to DC staff only (any dept)

Supervisor: full dept ticket/task visibility + assign to members + dept metrics

Member: sees own assigned tickets/tasks only

→ back and forth until both accept · every dept 2 meetings/week compulsory

CLAIMS: Staff = log meeting/event attendance → claim · Teacher = hour-based timesheet
