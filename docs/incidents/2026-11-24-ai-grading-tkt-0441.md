# Question Solver AI grading: how it works, how it broke, how to fix it

Written 26 Nov 2026 from ticket TKT-0441. Standing owner rule: **the grader must cost nothing. Never switch it to a paid model without asking.**

## 1. How grading works
1. The website (`app/api/mcq/[...path]/route.js`) proxies to the extraction service on the owner's PC (port 5178, behind a Cloudflare tunnel). The service is kept alive by `scripts/ops/question-solver-supervisor.sh`: kill its pid and it restarts within seconds. Never start a second copy by hand (EADDRINUSE).
2. A written answer goes to `POST /api/grade-structured-question` (async: a job id, then poll `grade-structured-status`, up to 6 minutes). The grader sends the mark scheme picture and the student's text to a model.
3. Order tried (`gradeStructuredQuestion` in `prototypes/mcq-digitizer/server.mjs`): every Google Gemini endpoint (one per key and model: 3 keys times the pool in `GEMINI_MODEL_POOL`, each with its own rate window), then one OpenRouter model (`OPENROUTER_MODEL`), then a last-resort lite Gemini model, flagged lowConfidence.
4. Settings live in `prototypes/mcq-digitizer/.env` (gitignored; keys `GEMINI_API_KEY_PRIMARY/SECONDARY/TERTIARY`, `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, `OPENROUTER_MODEL`, `GEMINI_MODEL_POOL`). Backups of the env file: `backups/mcq-digitizer-env-*.bak`.

## 2. Incident report, 24 to 26 Nov 2026 (TKT-0441)
- **Report:** "the AI grading is not working, it is not grading" (owner, 24 Nov).
- **Cause found:** the service's own health page `GET <tunnel>/api/grading-health` showed every Gemini model on all three keys failing with 503 "This model is currently experiencing high demand" or a 40 second timeout (only one success in the whole list), and the last-resort OpenRouter model `qwen/qwen3.8-27b:free` failing with 404 "unavailable for free". So when Google refuses, nothing is left. Google's overload comes in bursts; the same answer graded fine in 20 seconds at other times.
- **What I got wrong, so it is not repeated:**
  1. I set the fallback to `google/gemma-4-31b-it:free` on the strength of one text-only ping. In the real grader, with Google switched off, every call got 429 (all free Gemma on OpenRouter is rate limited).
  2. I then chose `anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5` on OpenRouter because it passed 3 of 3 (10 s, full breakdown) and said it was cheap. It is NOT free (about 0.1 cent per grading). The owner said: no cost, free only. Reverted.
  3. A first attempt at a second test copy used `pkill -f` with the port in the command line and killed my own shell. Kill test copies by checking `/proc/<pid>/environ`.
- **Final fix (26 Nov):** free only.
  - `GEMINI_MODEL_POOL` widened to 13 free Google models (39 routes): gemini-3.5-flash, 3-flash-preview, 3.6-flash, 3.7-flash, 3.8-flash, gemini-flash-latest, gemma-4-31b-it, gemma-4-26b-a4b-it, gemini-4-hydrogen, then the lite models 3.5-flash-lite, 3.1-flash-lite, 3.1-flash-lite-preview, flash-lite-latest.
  - `OPENROUTER_MODEL=openrouter/free` (OpenRouter's free auto-router), no cost.
  - `server.mjs`: the new lite names are flagged lowConfidence and have the lite rate limits.
  - Live check through the website: 2 of 2 gradings completed (27 s and 136 s).
- **Known weakness:** different models can give different marks for the same answer (one run 1/7, another 0/7). Lite and auto-routed answers carry lowConfidence. Treat grading as a guide; self-marking (TKT-0442) and the teacher check (TKT-0445) exist for that reason.

## 3. Which models are free (tested with the owner's keys, 26 Nov 2026)
Read it from the reply, not from blog posts (they disagree):
- **404 "no longer available to new users"**: retired (gemini-2.5-flash, 2.5-flash-lite, 2.5-pro).
- **429 "exceeded your current quota, check your plan and billing"**: no free quota, paid only (gemini-3.1-pro-preview, gemini-pro-latest, gemini-4-argon, -carbon, -neon, gemini-omni-*).
- **503 "high demand"**: the model exists and has a free quota but Google is busy; keep it in the pool.
- **200**: works now (gemini-3.5-flash-lite and gemini-flash-lite-latest answered correctly at the time of the test).
- Free OpenRouter vision models in the real grader with Google off: nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl:free 0 of 3 (empty JSON), openrouter/free 2 of 3, google/gemma-4-31b-it:free and the other Gemma 0 (429). Paid and rejected: qwen/qwen3.8-flash (unreliable JSON), anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5 (works, costs).
- Google lists models per key with `GET https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/models?pageSize=200&key=...` (49 generateContent models on 26 Nov). Limits are per project, not per key; Google does not publish exact free numbers. The server's table `GEMINI_LIMITS_BY_MODEL` is the owner's own screenshot of AI Studio limits (Flash 5 per minute, 20 per day; Flash-Lite 15 per minute, 500 per day).

## 4. Runbook when a student says grading does not work
1. Check Sentry first (de.sentry.io, org funteck, project divergencie-web) and the service health: get the tunnel from `GET /api/mcq-config`, then `GET <tunnel>/api/grading-health` (ok and fail counts, last error, cooldown per route).
2. 503 everywhere = Google busy: wait, it clears; grading retries across 39 routes. 429 = quota, a route sleeps for its cooldown. A dead OpenRouter model = 404 in the health page.
3. Test the real grader without touching the live one: run a copy on another port with Google blanked, `PORT=5188 GEMINI_API_KEY_PRIMARY= GEMINI_API_KEY_SECONDARY= GEMINI_API_KEY_TERTIARY= GEMINI_API_KEY= OPENROUTER_MODEL=<model> node-qs server.mjs`, then grade through `POST /api/grade-structured-question` with `async:true`. Script used: `data/ops-work/sched/free_fallback_test.sh <model>`.
4. Change models only in `.env`, then kill the service pid (the supervisor restarts it). Add a note to TKT-0441 and a CHANGELOG line. Do not spend money.
5. If it is still bad, tell the student to use Mark it myself (End exam, then Mark it myself).
