### Added
- Question Solver answer check, A Level (TKT-0362, TKT-0363): `scripts/ops/check_text_keys.py` reads the plain-text answer key ("1. A  2. B ...") of each A Level mark scheme again with its own parser and compares it with the stored answers. 6,521 of 6,523 agree. The 2 that did not were questions with no stored answer (Chemistry Atoms, Molecules and Stoichiometry Q33 and Q34), now filled in from the key (D and A). Gemini was not needed for the A Level answers.
- Question Solver answer check (TKT-0362, TKT-0363): both checkers now find a question on a mark scheme page that starts with the Save My Exams footer, and on a page that holds two questions, so about 100 more answers can be checked (the check had skipped them). One more Chemistry answer corrected (Property of Metals Q15, B to D) and 33 more IGCSE answers fixed from Gemini second opinions, each after reading the page.
- Question Solver, Solved practical papers (TKT-0379): the picker has a third Paper Type, "Solved practical papers", next to Topical worksheets and Yearly past papers. It lists worked solutions, compiled mark schemes, Cambridge practical booklets and examiner comments for A Level Physics and Chemistry Paper 3 and Paper 5 (31 documents, chosen by hand from the E: drive by `scripts/ops/build_solved_library.py`) and opens the PDF in a new tab; nothing is marked or tracked. A component with no topical worksheets now opens on a kind it has (yearly or solved) instead of an empty list, says what else it has, and changing board, subject or component resets the kind. The service serves the list at `/api/solved-library` (no file paths) and each document by id at `/api/solved-pdf`.
- Question Solver answer check (TKT-0362, TKT-0363): `scripts/ops/read_circled_letters.py` finds the circle on a mark scheme page and the option letter inside it (no model), `scripts/ops/gemini_check_answers.py` asks Gemini which option a page marks (57 of 57 right against answers checked by eye), and `scripts/ops/circled_crops.py` now finds text pages that start with the question number. The circle check read about 1,365 answers across all sources: 28 disagreed, and after reading each page 17 stored answers were wrong (14 `text-parsing`, 3 `gemini-4page`, one of them marked `visual-verified`) and were corrected, plus one more Physics answer found earlier. Some Save My Exams pages are wrong themselves (a lime kiln page circles D while its own text says calcium oxide), so no answer changes without a person reading the page.
- New UI, Question Solver, progress screens (TKT-0364, TKT-0365, TKT-0366, TKT-0367, TKT-0368, TKT-0369): the paper picker now says beside each paper whether you have not tried it, practised it, or your best score (full marks, 70% or more, or under 70% marked "worth another go"), and counts solved worksheets in each chapter. Progress has a new Completion tab (worksheets and yearly papers per subject: solved, tried, to go, or all solved, with the questions right on your best Test of each paper). The History tab is now "My attempts" and opens a paper's answers in a side panel instead of at the bottom of the page; the filters are compact. The score chart is a smooth curve with a shaded area, a dashed 70% line, your best score marked, your latest score labelled, a one-line trend, and text that stays readable on a phone.
- New UI, Question Solver, progress rules (TKT-0366, TKT-0367, TKT-0369): `ui2/features/solver/progressLogic.ts` works out, for each paper, whether the student has not tried it, only practised it, scored under 70% (worth another go), scored 70% or more, or got full marks, and for each subject how many worksheets and yearly papers are tried and solved. Ten unit tests. Screens follow in the next commits.
- Question Solver answer check (TKT-0362, TKT-0363): `scripts/ops/read_marked_answers.py` reads the green tick and red crosses on picture-only mark scheme pages, and `scripts/ops/circled_crops.py` cuts a strip round each page's colour marks so a person can read the circled letter. All 361 answers from the weak reader were checked this way. About 7 in 100 were wrong (26 fixed; the Chemistry worksheets that read C by default were the worst), corrected in the library with source `manual-fix-TKT-0362`. The earlier pixel reader that failed is removed.
- New UI, tickets: a quiet camera button in the corner of every signed-in page takes a picture of exactly what you are looking at (the button itself and the dialog are left out), records the page, window size, theme, language, time zone and browser, and opens the report dialog ready for the description. The report dialog can also attach a picture from a file or paste one (shrunk on your device, then to at most 1280 px and quality 60 on the server). New "My tickets" button in the top bar and menu: everyone sees their own tickets, the replies (theirs and support's) and the resolution, and can reply; internal notes and device details are never shown to them. Management's ticket panel shows the conversation, the screenshot, a "Screen details" box, the internal notes kept apart and labelled hidden from the sender, and a reply box. Classic UI is unchanged.
- Tickets, picture, device details and replies (API): a ticket can carry one picture (JPG, PNG or WebP; scaled to at most 1280 px and saved as a JPEG at quality 60, about 60 to 120 KB, in the private storage bucket under `tickets/`) and the details of the screen it was raised on (page, screen size, theme, browser, build). `GET /api/tickets` now works for everyone: Management gets all tickets as before; anyone else gets only their own, with the replies and the resolution text but never internal notes, the on-hold reason or device details. `POST /api/tickets` with `{ticketId, reply}` adds a reply that the sender and Management can both read (a sender cannot reply to a closed ticket). `GET /api/tickets?image=TKT-0001` gives a 5-minute link to the picture, for Management or the sender. What a sender may see is decided in one place, `lib/ticketView.js`. `GET /api/tickets?mine=1` returns only the tickets the caller raised themselves, in sender view, whatever their role (for a "My tickets" screen).
- Recordings dataset, syllabus chapters (TKT-0358): each recording's chapter is assigned by a person, not by a program: `docs/recording-chapter-assignments.json` holds the hand-made decisions (a topic rule per named recording and one entry per file read from its spoken intro; duplicates and non-class recordings are marked). `recording_syllabus.py` only looks chapters up in the Syllabus Viewer's data. Pilot: 5 recordings, all stayed unlisted.
