Built by Toppers,
For Toppers

DivergenCIE was founded on one belief: every student has the potential to reach the top — they just need the right system, mentors, and community behind them.

From One Student's
Frustration to a Global Movement

DivergenCIE was born out of frustration — a Cambridge student who struggled to find quality, personalised coaching for IGCSE and A Level exams. The tutors were either too expensive, too generic, or too disconnected from the actual exam system.

So we built something different. A coaching platform designed by exam-takers, for exam-takers. Every method, every resource, every teacher is chosen with one goal: get our students to the top of the world leaderboard.

Today, DivergenCIE students span Malaysia, India, Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, the UK, and beyond — with World and Country Toppers in IGCSE, A Level, AP, IB, SAT, and IELTS.

What Drives Us

Mission, Vision & Values

Every decision at DivergenCIE flows from these three pillars.

Our Mission

To make world-class Cambridge, AP, IB, and SAT coaching accessible to every motivated student — regardless of geography or background — and help them achieve top results that open doors globally.

Our Vision

To become the most recognised online coaching brand for international exams, known globally for producing World Toppers, A* cohorts, and students admitted to Oxford, Cambridge, LSE, Imperial, and the world's elite universities.

Our Values

Results over rhetoric. Radical transparency with students and parents. Continuous improvement in teaching methods. Genuine care for every student's journey — not just their grade.
