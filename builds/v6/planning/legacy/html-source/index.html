Got questions?

We've heard them all.
Here's the truth.

We cover the full stack — IGCSE, A Level, AP, IB, SAT, IELTS, and TOEFL. Subjects include Maths, Sciences, English, Economics, and more. If your exam board is Cambridge Assessment International Education or CollegeBoard, you're in the right place.

Live classes run via Zoom with interactive MS Whiteboard. Every session is recorded and auto-published to your student portal after class — so missing one is never a crisis. You also get class notes, past papers, and a full assignment tracker via Google Classroom.

Fully global. Our students are in Malaysia, the UK, India, Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, and beyond. Classes run across timezones and your schedule is displayed in your local time automatically. If you can open Zoom, you're in.

Try before you commit — take a Free Mock Exam and see what our teaching actually feels like. Then look at our results wall: country toppers, A*s across Cambridge and CollegeBoard, students admitted to Oxford, Cambridge, LSE, Imperial, and beyond. The receipts speak for themselves.

We offer tiered packages based on subject, exam board, and session frequency. Payment methods vary by region — we support Stripe, FPX, DuitNow, PayTM, EasyPaisa, Wise, and more. The pricing page detects your region and surfaces the right options automatically.

Every class is recorded and accessible immediately after. If you need to reschedule, raise a support ticket from your portal and we'll sort it — one click, no WhatsApp chasing. We also send missed-class alerts so nothing slips through.

Our target is 2 hours during working hours. Send us a message on WhatsApp or fill the enquiry form — you'll get an auto-reply instantly with next steps, and a human response fast. We don't leave people hanging.
